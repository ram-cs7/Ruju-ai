# Ruju.ai Go-To-Market Strategy & Final Deployment Steps

Ruju.ai is now fully functional, heavily branded, monetized, and ready to generate revenue. Below are the final technical deployment steps and a strategic playbook for gaining profits in the B2B SaaS space.

## 1. Final Deployment Checklist (What is pending?)

Technically, the code is 100% complete. To go live, you just need to move your API keys from development to production.

- [ ] **Buy a Domain**: Purchase `ruju.ai` (or similar) on Vercel, Namecheap, or Cloudflare.
- [ ] **Deploy to Vercel**: Push your code to GitHub and connect it to Vercel. 
- [ ] **Stripe Live Keys**: Go to the Stripe Dashboard, switch out of "Test Mode", create your $7/mo Product, and grab the **Live Secret Key** and **Live Price ID**. Put these in Vercel's Environment Variables.
- [ ] **Stripe Webhooks**: Add your new Vercel domain to Stripe Webhooks (e.g. `https://ruju.ai/api/webhook`) so Stripe can talk to your production database when people pay.
- [ ] **Clerk Production**: Switch Clerk to Production mode and add your new domain. Update the Clerk Keys in Vercel.
- [ ] **Supabase Production**: Grab your Supabase Postgres connection string and put it in Vercel. Run `npx prisma db push` against the live database from your local machine.

## 2. Who is the Target Customer? (The ICP)

Do not market this to generic ChatGPT users. You need to target professionals whose **jobs depend on accuracy and where hallucinations cause them to lose money or get fired.**

1. **Lawyers & Paralegals**: They read massive contracts. If AI hallucinates a clause, they can get sued. Ruju.ai's neon highlighter provides the exact evidence they need to trust the AI.
2. **Financial Analysts**: They ingest 100-page earnings reports. If an AI hallucinates a revenue number, they make a bad trade. 
3. **Journalists & Fact Checkers**: They have strict editorial standards and need to verify claims against raw interview transcripts.
4. **Compliance & Risk Officers**: They audit internal company documents to ensure policies are being met.

## 3. How to Sell and Market (The Growth Strategy)

### Strategy A: The "Trojan Horse" Share Link
Your most powerful marketing tool is already built into the app: **The Share Button**.
- **How it works:** An analyst uses Ruju.ai to verify a massive PDF. They click "Share" and send the `ruju.ai/share/123` link to their manager or a client to prove their work.
- **The Catch:** The manager opens the beautiful, interactive dossier. They see the neon highlights and the high trust score. At the top of the page, they see a button: *"Generated by Ruju.ai - Try it for free."* 
- **The Result:** The product markets itself organically every time a user shares a report.

### Strategy B: Cold Emailing with Niche Examples
Don't sell "AI". Sell the "Anti-Hallucination Engine".
- **Find** local mid-size law firms or accounting firms on LinkedIn.
- **Message:** *"Hi [Name], I noticed your team processes a lot of M&A contracts. Standard AI hallucinates on these, which is a massive liability. We built Ruju.ai—an AI pipeline that strictly refuses to answer unless it can highlight the exact clause in your document as evidence. Want 50 free verifications to test it out?"*

### Strategy C: The "Trust Audit" Lead Magnet
- Write a blog post or LinkedIn article titled: *"I fed the same contract to ChatGPT and Ruju.ai. ChatGPT hallucinated 3 clauses. Ruju highlighted the exact truth."*
- Show screenshots of your incredible dark-mode UI with the neon green highlights exposing a lie. Visual proof sells B2B software better than anything else.

You are currently charging **$7/month**. 

> [!TIP]
> **The B2B Pricing Rule:** If your software saves a lawyer or analyst just *one hour* of manual document reading per month, it is worth at least $50.
